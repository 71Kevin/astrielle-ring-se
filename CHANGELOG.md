# Changelog

## 1.0 — 2026-10-23

First version.

### Added
- Astrielle Ring (slot 36): a band of blackened silver with golden stars and grooves inlaid along it, a six-pointed
  golden star setting with six claws and a star ruby. Weight 0.25, value 3000; it cannot be disenchanted.
- Astrielle's Grace, a constant enchantment with 32 effects: health, magicka and stamina regenerate 100% faster;
  skills improve 30% faster; One-Handed +40% damage, Light Armor skill +40; Two-Handed, Archery and Block +30%;
  spells of all five schools cost 25% less and are 25% stronger (Alteration and Conjuration: last 25% longer);
  Sneak +30%; Speech skill +30; prices 25% better; sell any item to any merchant; Alchemy, Smithing and Enchanting
  +25%; magic resistance +25%; movement 15% faster; carry weight +100; no falling damage; equipped weapons recharge
  15 points per second; 20% shorter shout cooldown.
- An inventory description: "An heirloom from Astrielle's grandfather. Its star ruby burns like her eyes. Guard it
  well."
- Worn models fitted to the index finger of the CBBE 3BA female hands and the vanilla male hands (with the weight
  slider), and a ground model with collision.
- 4K textures: ring 4096 × 2048 px, gem 2048 × 2048 px, BC7 with mipmaps.
- The ring lies on a table in the Sleeping Giant Inn, Riverwood, and belongs to the player.
